Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: September 18, 2026

How to Stop Ryu Social Analytics From Accessing Your Account

If your account is connected to Ryu Social Analytics and you want to stop that connection, revoke its access directly through the platform you connected:

  • X: Settings and privacy → Security and account access → Apps and sessions → Connected apps → find the Ryu Social Analytics app → Revoke access.
  • Instagram / Meta: Settings → Apps and Websites (or the Accounts Center's "Apps and websites" section) → find the Ryu Social Analytics app → Remove.
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage apps that can access your account → find the Ryu Social Analytics app → Remove.

Revoking access through the platform immediately stops Ryu Social Analytics from making any further API calls using your account's token. It does not, by itself, delete data already collected before you revoked access — see the next section to request that separately.

How to Request Deletion of Data Already Collected

To request that data Ryu Social Analytics has already collected from your connected account be deleted, contact us at:

rynskymch01@gmail.com

Please include only:

  • The platform your account is on (X, Instagram, or TikTok)
  • Your account's username/handle on that platform, so we can locate the relevant records

We do not need, and you should not send us, any other personal information (for example, your platform password, government ID, or payment information) to process a deletion request.

We will confirm receipt of your request and the outcome of it (deletion completed, or an explanation if any part of it cannot be completed) within 30 days.

What Gets Deleted

Upon a verified deletion request, we will delete:

  • The stored OAuth access token and refresh token associated with your account
  • Post and metric records collected from your account through Ryu Social Analytics's API connections

We may retain aggregated or de-identified statistics that no longer identify your specific account, for internal historical analysis purposes, consistent with our Privacy Policy.
