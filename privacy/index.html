Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 18, 2026

1. What Ryu Social Analytics Is

Ryu Social Analytics ("we," "us," "the Service") is an internal social media analytics tool operated for the purpose of analyzing the social media presence of FRESH CHARM — its official group account(s) and the personal accounts of its members and staff who have chosen to connect their accounts. Ryu Social Analytics is not a consumer-facing product: it is not offered to, and does not accept connections from, members of the general public. Only accounts explicitly authorized by their own owners, on behalf of FRESH CHARM's management team, are connected to the Service.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Ryu Social Analytics accesses from connected social media platforms, how that information is used and stored, and the rights available to individuals whose accounts are connected.

2. Information We Collect

Ryu Social Analytics connects to social media platforms via each platform's own official developer API, using OAuth authorization granted directly by the account owner. We only request the minimum set of permissions ("scopes") needed to read public post content and performance metrics. We do not request, and do not use, any permission to post, comment, like, follow, or otherwise write to a connected account.

Depending on which platform an account owner has connected, the following categories of information may be collected:

2.1 Social Media Account Information

  • Public profile identifiers (e.g., username/handle, platform-assigned account ID)
  • Public account-level statistics (e.g., follower count, following count, lifetime like count, total video/post count)

2.2 Social Media Content

  • Public post identifiers, URLs, and publish timestamps
  • Public post text/caption
  • Public post media metadata (e.g., video duration, cover image URL, media type)

2.3 Analytics / Performance Metrics

  • Public engagement metrics associated with posts (e.g., view count, like count, comment count, share count, impression count, reach)
  • For the account owner's own posts, and only where the platform's API makes this available exclusively to the authenticated account owner, certain non-public metrics (e.g., link clicks, profile visits, average watch time) may also be collected.

2.4 Access Tokens and Refresh Tokens

To maintain the connection an account owner has authorized, we store the OAuth access token and refresh token issued by the platform. These tokens are credentials, not personal content — see Section 7 (Data Security) for how they are protected.

2.5 What We Do Not Collect

We do not collect private messages, private account data the account owner has not authorized, or data belonging to accounts other than those explicitly connected by their owners. We do not collect data about people who merely view, like, or comment on a connected account's public posts. We do not currently collect any data from YouTube or Google services — see Section 8 (Third-Party Services) for how this policy will be updated if that changes.

3. OAuth Authorization

Every connection between a social media account and Ryu Social Analytics is established through that platform's own OAuth authorization flow. The account owner is shown, and must explicitly grant, the specific scopes requested. No account is connected without this explicit, platform-mediated consent step, and no account owner's login credentials (password) are ever seen, requested, or stored by Ryu Social Analytics — only the token(s) issued by the platform after authorization.

4. How We Use Information

Information collected through connected accounts is used solely to:

  • Detect and record new posts from connected accounts
  • Track how those posts' performance metrics change over time
  • Produce internal reports, dashboards, and analysis for FRESH CHARM's own management and content strategy decisions
  • Compare performance across platforms and across pieces of content, for FRESH CHARM's own internal use

We do not use collected data to serve advertising (including retargeting or interest-based advertising), and we do not use it for any purpose unrelated to providing or improving the internal analytics features described above.

5. AI-Assisted Internal Analysis

Some of the internal reports and analysis described in Section 4 are produced with the help of an AI assistant tool, currently Claude (provided by Anthropic), accessed through Claude Code / the Anthropic API. Where this is used:

  • The AI assistant reads previously-collected data described in Section 2 (post content, metrics, account statistics) in order to summarize it, identify patterns, and help draft the internal reports described in Section 4. It is used as a tool by FRESH CHARM's own operating team, in the same way one might use spreadsheet or business-intelligence software — it is not an independent party that receives data for its own separate purposes.
  • We do not use collected data to train, fine-tune, or otherwise develop any AI/ML model, whether our own or a third party's.
  • As of this policy, data submitted through Claude Code / the Anthropic API is governed by Anthropic's own commercial API terms, which — at the time this policy was written — do not use API customer content to train Anthropic's models by default. This is subject to Anthropic's own terms of service and privacy policy in effect at the time of processing, which we encourage reviewing separately for full detail.
  • This is the only third-party AI processing currently used. If we begin using a different or additional AI service, this section will be updated before that change takes effect.

6. Data Storage

Collected data is stored locally, on infrastructure operated by FRESH CHARM / Ryu Social Analytics's own operating team. As of the date of this policy, this consists of local files (not a public-facing database or third-party cloud data warehouse) and a locally-maintained spreadsheet used as the internal record of truth. Data is not stored on, or synchronized to, any third-party consumer cloud storage service. If this storage architecture changes in the future (for example, if a hosted database is introduced), this section will be updated before that change takes effect.

7. Data Security

Access tokens, refresh tokens, and application credentials are stored using the operating system's secure credential storage (macOS Keychain) and are never written to source control, log files, or any document shared outside the operating team. Access to the systems that store connected-account data is limited to FRESH CHARM / Ryu Social Analytics's own personnel.

8. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade any data obtained through connected social media accounts to third parties, including advertisers, data brokers, or information resellers.

We do not transfer collected data to any third party except:

  • The AI-assisted analysis described in Section 5.
  • Where required to comply with a legal obligation, protect the rights and safety of FRESH CHARM or others, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or similar transaction involving FRESH CHARM's operations — in each case only to the extent necessary and, where feasible, with notice.

Ryu Social Analytics connects to the following platforms via their official developer APIs, each also governed by that platform's own terms and privacy practices in addition to this policy:

  • X (Twitter) — subject to the X Developer Agreement and X's own Privacy Policy.
  • Instagram / Meta — subject to Meta's Platform Terms and Meta's own Privacy Policy.
  • TikTok — subject to the TikTok for Developers Terms of Service and TikTok's own Privacy Policy.

Future platforms: this policy is written so it can extend to additional platforms Ryu Social Analytics may connect to in the future (for example, YouTube). No such additional platform is connected as of the date of this policy. If we add a new platform integration, we will update this section and Section 2 before that integration goes live, and — where the new platform's own developer policies require additional disclosures (for example, Google's API Services User Data Policy for a future YouTube integration) — we will update this policy to meet those requirements before connecting any account on that platform.

9. Data Retention

We retain data obtained through a connected account for as long as that account remains connected, and thereafter for internal recordkeeping and historical performance analysis, unless the account owner requests earlier deletion under Section 12. Upon a verified deletion request, we delete the specific records described in Section 12; we may retain aggregated or de-identified statistics that no longer identify a specific account.

10. User Rights

Anyone whose account is connected to Ryu Social Analytics has the right to:

  • Know what categories of data have been collected from their connected account (Section 2)
  • Revoke Ryu Social Analytics's access to their account at any time (Section 11)
  • Request deletion of data collected from their account (Section 12)
  • Ask questions about how their data is used, via the contact details in Section 13

11. Revoking Authorization

An account owner may revoke Ryu Social Analytics's access to their account at any time, directly through the connected platform's own settings:

  • X: Settings and privacy → Security and account access → Apps and sessions → Connected apps
  • Instagram / Meta: Settings → Apps and Websites (or the equivalent Accounts Center "Apps and websites" section)
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage apps that can access your account

Revoking access through the platform immediately prevents Ryu Social Analytics from making further API calls on that account's behalf using the associated token. It does not, by itself, delete data already collected before revocation — see Section 12 for how to request that separately, and see our separate Data Deletion Instructions page for step-by-step guidance.

12. Data Deletion

An account owner may request deletion of data Ryu Social Analytics has collected from their connected account by contacting us using the details in Section 13, or by following our Data Deletion Instructions page.
