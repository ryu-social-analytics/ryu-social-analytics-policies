Ryu Social Analytics

A social media analytics application that helps FRESH CHARM understand how its content performs across the social media platforms it uses.

What Ryu Social Analytics Is

Ryu Social Analytics is a social media analytics application built to support FRESH CHARM's social media operations. It connects, through each platform's own official developer API and OAuth authorization, to social media accounts owned by FRESH CHARM and by FRESH CHARM's members/staff, in order to read public post content and performance data and turn it into internal reports and analysis that inform FRESH CHARM's content and social media strategy.

What It Does

  • Detects new posts published by a connected account.
  • Tracks performance metrics over time — for example, views, likes, comments, and shares — for posts from a connected account.
  • Records account-level statistics such as follower counts.
  • Produces internal analysis and reports comparing how content performs, to support FRESH CHARM's own decision-making.

Ryu Social Analytics is read-only: it does not post, comment, like, follow, upload, or otherwise write to any connected account, on any platform.

Who It Is For

Ryu Social Analytics is used by FRESH CHARM's management to analyze FRESH CHARM's own official social media presence and the personal accounts of members/staff who have chosen to connect their accounts. Every connected account belongs to FRESH CHARM or to a FRESH CHARM member/staff account owner who has personally completed that platform's OAuth authorization for their own account.

Supported Platforms

Ryu Social Analytics currently connects to:

  • X (Twitter)
  • Instagram
  • TikTok

See the TikTok Integration section below for TikTok-specific detail. We may add support for additional platforms in the future; our Privacy Policy will be updated before any new platform integration goes live.

TikTok Integration

On TikTok, Ryu Social Analytics uses TikTok Login Kit to obtain OAuth authorization from the owner of a TikTok account, and TikTok's Display API to read that account's public videos and account statistics. Specifically:

  • The account owner signs in to TikTok and explicitly grants the requested permissions through TikTok's own OAuth consent screen.
  • Ryu Social Analytics reads the authorized account's public video list and per-video metrics (for example, view, like, comment, and share counts) and account statistics (for example, follower count).
  • Ryu Social Analytics does not post, upload, or otherwise write to a TikTok account — it only reads data the account owner has authorized.
  • Authorization can be revoked by the account owner at any time from TikTok's own settings; see our Data Deletion Instructions.
